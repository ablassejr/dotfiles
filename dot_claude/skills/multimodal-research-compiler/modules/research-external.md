# Research external sources

Use external research for primary prior art, papers, current facts, and alternatives not answered by local or official sources.

Search from a declared subquestion and required claim type. Prefer primary sources, standards, original papers, vendor documentation, and first-party implementation reports. Use secondary sources to discover primary material or to represent a clearly attributed interpretation.

Record publication date, event date, version, jurisdiction or environment, author or owning body, and retrieval date when they affect applicability. Compare independent sources rather than counting duplicated syndication as corroboration. Respect quotation and licensing limits; store locators and concise evidence, not unnecessary copies.

Stop when the research gate is satisfied. Do not broaden the topic merely because adjacent sources are interesting.
