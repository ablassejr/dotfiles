# Supervisor design proposal

Prepare one coherent proposal at the scope's existing final review. Its purpose is to explain the recommendation, intended behavior, consequential tradeoffs, and actual open choices to a person without prior conversation. Use `$design-document` and [review and artifact design](review-and-artifact-design.md), including purposeful media, complete context, restrained prompts, and the capability-aware verification fallback.

Reuse the proposal's selected document home: repository documentation, a shared document, wiki, tracker document, or portable draft. Resolve the actual project and reader rather than assuming a named provider. A missing destination does not prevent preparation. Request a target only when external publication is needed and no prior instruction or established association supplies it.

Open with a descriptive project-and-role title, purpose, audience, status, and governing source. Explain the design in complete sentences and purposeful visuals. Keep supporting inventories and detailed evidence behind optional descriptive references. Do not duplicate the full narrative in a specification store, separate executive summary, or handoff. The proposal must remain understandable without opening another record.

At semantic scope, explain the current specification. At program scope, explain the actual team's ownership, issue outcomes, prerequisites, and handoffs. At ticket scope, explain its bounded design and rationale. These scopes retain distinct approval authority while reusing settled decisions. Neither the document provider nor the number of available connectors determines the artifact count.

Before an authorized write, inspect the existing target and prepare the concrete content and media. After writing, fetch and compare title, content, source binding, associations, and intended access. Inspect rendering and controls with available permitted tools. Static checks and content hashes do not prove layout or playback; disclose unavailable checks and preserve an expressly required verification gate.

Bind the existing final review to the exact prepared or fetched revision and its source. Explain material revisions in the review message while keeping the document a coherent statement of the current proposal. Reuse unchanged approval when its binding permits it; changed semantics return to their owner. Do not create another human approval for ordinary editorial repair or the choice of publishing medium.

The [Linear/Camunda publication adapter](linear-supervisor-design-document.md) describes its native parent types and `linear-design-proposal-v1` result. Use it only when that workflow is selected. Another provider uses its own supported publishing and revision evidence; do not manufacture a Linear binding for it.
