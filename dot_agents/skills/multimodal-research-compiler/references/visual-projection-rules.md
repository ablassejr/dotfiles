# Visual projection rules

Every visual element must reference Research Model identifiers or be clearly marked as explanatory decoration. A diagram must not introduce a relationship, chronology, boundary, confidence, or recommendation absent from the model.

Encode explicit rationale, inferred rationale, expired constraints, current invariants, contradictions, unknown provenance, and human decisions with distinguishable labels or styles. Do not let color, size, placement, or polish imply stronger confidence than the underlying claim.

Use stable `view_id`, `research_release`, source-claim references, and relevant basis or decision references. Validate formal structure before producing a polished frame when both are required. Read the completed visual back, compare its claims and emphasis with the model, and update the model before regenerating a meaning-changing correction.
