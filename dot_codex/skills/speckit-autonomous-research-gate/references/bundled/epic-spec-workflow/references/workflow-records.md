# Workflow records and handoff

Keep enough durable evidence to reconstruct the current scope without making operational bookkeeping the team's reading experience. Record scope and owner when established, approved basis identity, authoritative specification revision, repository baseline when relevant, material decisions, source and evidence references, review outcomes, exact approval bindings, incomplete writes, and the next unresolved action. Use existing project records and purposeful sections instead of a new document for every field.

The design proposal owns the explanation. The specification owns approved semantics. The delivery plan owns task scope, ownership, and dependencies. Evidence answers a particular claim or provenance question. A handoff names the exact records and their verification rather than repeating their narratives. These roles can share a document when their authority is clear.

Tie a human response to the actual question, its revision and reviewed content, and the person and scope granting approval. Preserve the original answer and its assessed consequences. Do not reuse a response after its material source binding changes. A question invalidated by evidence remains in history while the parent recomputes affected choices. A provider identifier is optional internal metadata, not an explanation for the reader.

An approved handoff identifies the exact specification snapshot and its source, basis lineage, relevant baseline, accepted decisions and constraints, unresolved nonblocking questions, scope exclusions, necessary evidence and views, applicable accounting obligations, approval, and completed verification. Use descriptive durable references appropriate to the selected destination. A repository commit and named document role can provide identity; the workflow does not require a Notion page or Linear document.

Do not call the handoff ready for a team until its required content, approval, freshness, and audience access are established. A portable unpublished package can be delivered as preparation with clear limits. Preserve canonical repository documentation and recovery records through cleanup.

For an existing Camunda process, use [the runtime evidence envelopes](camunda-workflow-records.md) without inventing or renaming provider-specific fields. Its execution snapshot is derived from the engine. These envelopes describe that adapter profile; ordinary authoring records do not need process IDs or fake user-task keys.
