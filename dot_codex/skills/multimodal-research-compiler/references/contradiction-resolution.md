# Contradiction resolution

Treat a contradiction as a research object with its own identifier, participating claim references, materiality, status, and resolution evidence.

First compare scope, time, version, environment, authority type, and whether each claim describes intent or observation. If those dimensions explain the difference, narrow both claims and record the reconciliation. If evidence is genuinely incompatible, seek a source able to distinguish them. If no permitted source can do so, bound the uncertainty and describe which conclusions it prevents.

A human may choose future behavior, accept risk, or define product semantics. A human decision does not retroactively make a disputed historical or factual claim true. Preserve the factual contradiction separately from the normative decision.

Route an evidence defect to the earliest stage that can repair it, then repeat only affected downstream synthesis and projection checks.
