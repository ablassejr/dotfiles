# Research gate

Return exactly one gate outcome with a reason and the affected question or claim references:

- `CONTINUE_RESEARCH` when a material factual gap is resolvable within the source boundary.
- `INVOKE_GROUNDING` when provenance or the continuing validity of a current mechanism must be reconstructed before the same question can be answered.
- `REQUEST_HITL` when the remaining issue is normative, risk-bearing, irreversible, or defines product semantics.
- `REVISE_FIRST_PRINCIPLES` when applicable evidence invalidates or materially changes the approved basis.
- `PROCEED_TO_SYNTHESIS` when material factual claims are sufficiently supported and remaining uncertainty cannot change the conclusion.
- `STOP_WITH_UNRESOLVED_GAP` when a material factual gap remains but further permitted research cannot resolve it.

Before synthesis, confirm that source versions and dates apply, current and intended state are distinct, every inference is labeled, contradictions are resolved or bounded, and unresolved judgments are assigned to the human. Do not continue gathering sources merely to increase volume after the gate is satisfied.
